# Substack Article Facilitator

Write better Substack articles, faster, without handing your voice to AI. The Substack Article Facilitator guides you from first idea to finished piece, grades it until it's ready to publish, and repurposes it for LinkedIn, so one article works on two platforms.

## Our approach

This isn't a tool for churning out AI slop. Your ideas, experience and stories stay at the centre of every piece. The AI is your editor and coach, not your ghostwriter.

## What's inside

One skill bundle with three skills that work together, plus a hook library.

| Skill | What it does | Use it when you say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Article Coach** | Runs the whole process: your author profile, article ideas, an interview to draw out your real stories, drafting, grading, image prompts and a ready-to-paste checklist | "Help me write an article", "I have an article idea", "What should I write about next?" |
| **Article Writer** | Drafts the article from your material: headline and opening first, then the body, a Substack version and a LinkedIn newsletter version | "Write an article about...", "Draft my LinkedIn newsletter on..." |
| **Article Grader** | Scores a draft out of 10 on headline, opening, structure, substance, momentum, voice, value and ending, runs a voice and format check, and gives the top 3 fixes | "Grade this article", "Is this ready to publish?" |

**Hook Library:** 50 proven headline and opening patterns in 12 categories, such as receipts, contrarian, confession and before and after. The writer uses them for its headline options, filled only with your real details.

**Author profile:** the first time you use the coach, it asks about your background, readers, call to action, UK or US spelling and writing rules. With your approval, it saves your answers as your own private profile: in your installed copy of the skill in the Claude app, as `author-profile.md` in your folder in Claude Code, or as a file you can add to a Claude Project. All three skills use it, so every article sounds like you. Your profile never goes back into the shared template.

**Profile updates:** at the end of each session, the coach asks: "Would you like me to summarise and update your profile to include things I've learned about you today?" It shows you the changes first and only saves them if you say yes.

## How to install

1. Download `substack-article-facilitator.zip` from this repository.
2. In Claude, open **Customize > Skills** and upload the zip.
3. Start a new chat and say "Help me write an article".

The Substack Article Facilitator works on its own. You don't need any other plugin.
